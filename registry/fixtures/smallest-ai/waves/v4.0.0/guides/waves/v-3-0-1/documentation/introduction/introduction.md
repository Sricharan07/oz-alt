# Introduction

> Waves is a platform to deliver real-time hyper-realistic text to speech.

## About Waves

Welcome to [Waves](https://app.smallest.ai/dashboard/text-to-speech), the cutting-edge platform designed to deliver real-time, hyper-realistic text-to-speech solutions. Our platform leverages advanced AI models to provide seamless and natural-sounding speech synthesis, catering to a wide range of applications including voicebots, interactive systems, and more.

### Key Features

* **Multiple Models**: Waves offers a variety of models, each optimized for different use cases. Whether you need ultra-fast response times or high emotional expressiveness, we have a model that fits your needs.
* **Accents and Languages**: Our models support various accents and languages, ensuring that your applications can communicate effectively with a global audience.
* **Real-Time Processing**: Experience near-instantaneous speech generation, making Waves ideal for real-time applications such as customer service bots and live interactive systems.
